Tuesday · August 25

Flume Gorge & Echo Lake

The nature day: a waterfall gorge in the morning, a mountain lake in the afternoon.

  1. Morning
    Breakfast in, sandwiches built Full-kitchen morning — make the picnic before the gorge.
  2. AM
    Flume Gorge The signature walk — waterfalls, boardwalks bolted to the walls; the full loop runs about two miles.
    Google Maps Apple Maps Tickets & hours
  3. Lunch
    Picnic in the notch Sandwiches from the apartment.
  4. PM
    Echo Lake Beach Swim first, then pedal boat or kayak — the last boats go out an hour before the beach closes.
    Google Maps Apple Maps Beach info
  5. Dinner
    Gordi’s Fish & Steak House 260 Main St — doors open 4:00 PM Tuesday; carved lobster out front.
    Google Maps Apple Maps gordisfishandsteak.com

The weather pivot

Tuesday is the most weather-sensitive day of the trip. If the sky turns, keep the gorge and the lake flexible rather than forcing the outdoor plan — the pools and the game room are the built-in backup.

Wednesday · August 26

Amusement day

The one big splurge: a full-day park, suits and shoes packed, and an easy evening afterward.

  1. All day
    The park Rides, water play, and as much soft serve as the day allows.
  2. Bring
    Suits, water shoes, change of clothes Both parks have water-play areas — plan for soaked, happy kids.
  3. Evening
    Keep it simple Easy dinner and resort time — no second act after a park day.
